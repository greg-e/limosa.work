# Brand Usage Checklist

Use this before publishing decks, one-pagers, landing pages, and pilot docs.

## Visual checks

- Primary logo selected for background (dark/light variant used correctly)
- Clear space preserved around logo
- Colors limited to approved palette
- Typography matches package hierarchy
- Visuals feel field-practical, not speculative/futuristic

## Message checks

- The page says this is research stage, with no product or customers
- Language is direct and plain
- The founder is not named

## Content checks

- Hardware is described as a plan: off-the-shelf hardware, our own software
- No price unless one has been published
- Do not describe GPS, truck tracking, a shop dock, or labor types as the product
