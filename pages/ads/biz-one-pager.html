Actual

For service businesses

Payroll and job costs from your crew's own records.

How it works

  1. Each worker wears an Actual badge they own. It records their time on each job site, with nothing to tap or clock into.
  2. Before payroll, each worker checks their hours and shares the record with you.
  3. You run payroll from those hours and see what each job cost, by the minute.

Who it's for

Landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, pest control, cleaning, in-home care, delivery and other businesses whose crews work at customers' properties.

What you get

  • Time on each job site for every worker who shares it with you.
  • Hours your crew has checked before they reach payroll.
  • Each job's crew time next to its quote, for the next bid.
  • No timecards to collect or retype.

Why crews wear it

The badge and the record belong to the worker. They use it to check their own pay, on every job they work.
