Example ads

These show how we would advertise Actual once the badge is available, to the crews who wear it and to the businesses they work for. Actual is in development, and the badge isn’t for sale yet. The hours in each ad are made-up examples.

For workers

Crews get a badge they own and a record of their own hours.

For businesses

Owners and payroll get checked hours and each job's crew time, shared by the crew.
