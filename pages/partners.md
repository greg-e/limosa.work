# Co-founders

Two roles are open.

## Technical co-founder

Hardware and software. The plan is off-the-shelf hardware and custom software, then a first paid pilot in roughly three to six months.

## Sales or industry co-founder

Someone who knows the owners of small service businesses: landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, pest control, house cleaning, in-home care, janitorial, delivery.

## Also useful

If you run a service business or work on a crew, a conversation about how you track time today helps. It is a conversation, not a pilot.

## Still open

Who pays for the device first, and what the final pitch should be.

Close competitors exist, including ZeroBadge, XGen, Momentum CrewID, Jobber, Workyard, and Timeero. The open gap is a truly zero-step badge, the whole day per person, and a record the worker trusts or owns.
