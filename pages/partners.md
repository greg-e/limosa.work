Actual is a single badge each worker wears. It records who worked, where they went, and what work they did, with no tags, GPS, cellular, phones, or paperwork. It's in development.

We have the problem, the architecture, and the design. Now we need partners: businesses that want to apply Actual to their work or their industry, and engineers who want to build it.

**Get started:** greg@limosa.work

# Actual Partners

---

## Industry Partners

Actual started in landscaping, but the same problem shows up anywhere crews work away from an office: construction, utilities, municipal public works, solar, facilities, and more. If proof of work matters in your business or your industry, we want to build it with you.

### What you bring
- A business or industry with field crews, and the problems they have proving time and work today
- People who know how that work is done
- A place to test: one crew, a shop or yard they start and end at, and willing workers

### What we do together
- Map how work in your industry looks to the badge's sensors: which tools, tasks, and movements matter
- Adapt the activity models and reports to your work
- Field-test prototypes with your crew once hardware exists

### What you get
- Early access to prototypes
- A stake or preferred terms in your industry, worked out together
- A product shaped around how your crews work
- Recognition as a founding partner

Not ready to commit? Thirty minutes telling us how your crews track time today still helps, and it costs nothing.

## Engineers

We're looking for engineers who want to own a piece of what they build.

### Who we're looking for
- **Embedded hardware / PCB:** low-power badge design with inertial, barometric, acoustic, and vibration sensing, in a rugged enclosure.
- **Firmware:** on-device capture, labor classification, local storage, offload at the shop, power management.
- **Sensor fusion / navigation / ML:** rebuilding routes and job sites from motion data without GPS, and recognizing labor from motion and vibration.
- **Cloud / data backend:** ingestion, road and elevation map matching, site discovery, tamper-proof storage.
- **Integrations:** connectors to payroll, job costing, scheduling, and billing systems.

### What you get
- A stake in what we build, with terms worked out together.
- A say in the architecture, starting with the first prototype.
- Recognition as a founding builder.

### What we ask
- Engineering time on a defined piece of the system.
- Regular check-ins and honest technical judgment, including telling us when the design is wrong.

We're not looking for passive investors. Every partner contributes engineering work or industry knowledge.
