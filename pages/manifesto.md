# The Actual Manifesto  
**Actual tells the truth about the work automatically, with no extra effort.**

Actual is built for the people who do the work and the people who support them. It starts from one simple idea:

**If the work matters, the record of the work should be true.**

Everything else follows from that.


### The person doing the work should never have to “prove” they worked  
Timecards, forms, photos, and check-ins steal time from the job and force people to explain themselves after the fact.

Actual puts the truth right where the work happens.  
You wear the badge. It records what really happened.  
No phone. No app. No extra steps.

The record starts with you.

---

### The badge is a witness  
The badge doesn’t spy. It doesn’t record conversations. It doesn’t monitor your day. It captures the basic facts of the job:

- When you arrived  
- When you moved  
- When you used tools  
- When the day wrapped up

It only records what the work creates. Nothing more.

---

### The truth should never be editable  
Once something happens, it shouldn’t get changed later.

Actual saves every record exactly the way it happened. If something needs to be clarified, the clarification gets added and the original stays. That way, nobody has to argue about time, work, or what’s “really” true.

---

### Tools should make life easier  
If a tool slows you down, it’s a burden.

The badge doesn’t need GPS, a cell signal, a phone, tags on your tools, or a learning curve. It records the day, and when it goes back in the dock at the shop, the day uploads on its own. You don’t have to manage it.

---

### Real data makes the whole job run smoother  
When the truth is automatic:

- Pay is clean  
- Schedules match reality  
- Estimates get better  
- Delays finally make sense  
- Nobody has to “remember” anything

Actual shows how the job really flowed, so nobody has to guess.

---

### Better decisions come from what actually happened  
Because the badge records the real story of the job, the system can show:

- Where time goes  
- Where things slow down  
- How time splits between driving, equipment, and hand work  
- Where the crew gets stuck  
- What work really costs

---

### Simple always wins  
Complicated systems break, confuse people, or get ignored.

Actual is built to stay simple:

- One badge  
- One worker  
- One automatic record of the truth

No forms, steps, or training.

---

### The promise  
Actual exists so the people who do the work finally get the benefit of the truth.

It protects your time.  
It protects your integrity.  
It protects the story of the job exactly as it happened.
