Privacy

This page covers what we collect today through this website, and how we plan for the badge to handle each worker's record.

Last updated November 16, 2026

This website

The site runs no analytics and sets no cookies. If you switch between light and dark mode, your browser remembers the choice on your device. GitHub hosts the site.

The survey

The survey stores only the answers you give. Every question is optional, including your name, company, email and phone. We use contact details only to follow up about the survey, and to tell you when the badge is ready to try if you check that box.

We don't store your IP address, your browser details, or the page you came from. To limit spam, the survey uses your IP address for an hourly count, kept only as a scrambled code that can't be read back as your address.

Answers are saved in a database we run on Cloudflare. If saving fails, the survey opens an email draft to us with your answers, and you decide whether to send it.

To see or delete your answers, write to greg@limosa.work.

The badge

Actual is in development and no one wears a badge yet. This is how we plan for it to work:

  • Each worker owns their record.
  • The worker checks their hours before sending them to the company they work for.
  • A business sees the record only with the worker's permission: where work happened, what was done and the minutes it took.
  • A worker can stop sharing with a business at any time and keeps their own record.

Before anyone wears a badge, this page will list exactly what it records.
