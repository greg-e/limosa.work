About Actual

The best record of a workday is one nobody has to fill in.

Actual makes a badge that field workers wear on the job. It records where work happens, what's being done, and how long it takes, with nothing to tap or clock into. The record belongs to the worker, who can check their hours before payroll. With the worker's permission, the business uses the same record to see what each job costs.

Actual is for the trades that work at their customers' properties, including landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, pest control, cleaning, in-home care, and delivery.

Our example ads show how we plan to advertise Actual to crews and the businesses they work for once the badge is available.

Questions? Write to greg@limosa.work, or tell us how you track time.

Our logo and domain come from the black-tailed godwit, Limosa limosa.

The godwit reads the sun, the stars and the earth's magnetic field against senses and a clock inside its own body. It flies thousands of miles between Iceland and Africa and lands on the same patch of ground it left months before. Pairs that spend the winter apart, one in Spain and one in West Africa, arrive back in Iceland within days of each other. Its cousin, the bar-tailed godwit, flies from Alaska to New Zealand in one nonstop flight of more than eleven days.

The badge is built on the same idea. It will combine what it senses around it with its own sensors to know where the work happened and how long it took, without anyone stopping to tell it.
