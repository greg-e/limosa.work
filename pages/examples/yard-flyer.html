Actual

Your hours, on a badge you own.

How it works

Wear the Actual badge on the job. It records your time on each job, with nothing to tap or clock into.

  • Check your hours before they go to payroll.
  • Match every pay stub to the time you worked.
  • Wear it for any company you work for, on any job.
  • Your employer sees the record only when you share it.

Who it's for

People in landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, pest control, cleaning, in-home care, delivery and similar work, on a crew or on their own. Anyone paid by the hour who wants their own record of the work.
