# Principles of Actual

## 1. Record Work Where It Happens  
Capture belongs on the worker, at the moment of work. Records made later from memory are guesses.

## 2. Ask Nothing of the Crew  
Every task added for the crew costs time and gets skipped. The badge does the recording; the crew does the work.

## 3. Keep the Record's Origin  
Every record shows who created it, when, and on which badge, all the way to payroll. Derived results sit beside the original record and never replace it.

## 4. Show How Work Flows  
A record of real days shows the sequence of work, its timing, and where it stalls.

## 5. Respect the Worker  
The record belongs to the person who did the work. Collect it with consent. Never use it to record conversations or watch behavior.
