# Principles of Actual

These are design criteria for an idea. Nothing has been built.

## 1. The record comes directly from the person
The badge is the worker's own verified timecard. The hours on it are the hours they will be paid for.

## 2. Every day, and the whole day
The record covers the property, the drive, and the shop. A partial day hides what the job really cost.

## 3. A product, not a service
The plan is off-the-shelf hardware and custom software. The thing the crew would wear is meant to be a product.

## 4. Inside the work relationship
One arrangement being explored: the worker owns the record, and the employer subscribes to it with the worker's permission.

## 5. It does not feel like policing
No daily steps. No phone. Nothing installed on the customer's property. The crew should want to wear it.

## 6. It does not change the work
It does not change how work is sold or how the day is organized. It shows what each job really cost.
