Where Actual stands

Actual is a research-stage idea. There is no product and there are no customers yet. This page lists what exists today and what's planned, and we update it when something changes.

Last updated November 16, 2026

WhatStatusDetails
Survey Live Ten questions for field crews and the people who run them about how they track time today. Take the survey.
Research Under way We're talking with crews, owners and office staff before we build anything.
Badge Planned Off-the-shelf hardware running our own software.
Worker's record Planned Each worker owns their record and checks their hours before sending them to the company they work for.
Business view Planned With the worker's permission, the business sees where work happened, what was done and the minutes it took.
First paid pilot Planned We're aiming for one in 3 to 6 months.
Customers, pilots, prices None yet We'll list them here when they exist.
Co-founders Looking A technical co-founder, and a co-founder from sales or the field trades.

How we handle survey answers and how the badge will handle a worker's record: Privacy.
