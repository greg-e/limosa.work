Truth from the field

Actual is a badge each worker wears. It records the day as it happens: location, task and time. There's nothing to tap or update. Each worker owns their verified record and checks their hours before sending them to the company they work for. With the worker's permission, the same record shows the business where work happened, what was done, and the exact minutes, so it can cost each job from the record. Actual is in development.

Nothing to remember. The badge records everything automatically.
