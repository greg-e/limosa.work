Proof of Work

A customer says nobody showed up. The van's GPS shows it parked for an hour. The timesheet says the job was done. When the work is questioned, can you prove it? A clock-in and clock-out show when the day started and ended. Everything in between is someone's word.

How Actual is solving the problem

Nothing to remember. A badge you wear records where you are and what you're doing at each job, as it happens.

Proof anyone can check. The record is sealed as it's made. Nobody can change it, so anyone you share it with can trust it.

Yours to share. The worker owns the badge and the record, and chooses who sees it.

One Record

Your Work

  • When your work is questioned, show what happened.
  • On payday, match your pay to your hours.
  • When you go for your next job, show a live resume: verified hours by type of work, from every employer.

Your Team's Work

  • When a customer disputes a visit or a bill, show the record.
  • Run payroll and invoices from hours your team has checked.
  • Bid the next job on what the last one took, and show customers a verified history of your work.

The principles behind Actual

Before building anything, we wrote down 63 ideas about work, pay and proof. Here are four, picked at random. See the whole deck.

  • Finance Labor costs are created when people and equipment move to jobs. Track the movement, and costs follow automatically. Cost Flows From Movement
  • Principles Information must be reliable enough for leadership to bet the business on it. Truth You Can Stand On
  • Flow A day's work should be reconstructable from its own records, with nothing left to memory or pieced together afterward. The Day Tells Its Story
  • Work Practice Workers care most about an accurate paycheck. A system that gets every hour right earns their trust. Get Paid Right
