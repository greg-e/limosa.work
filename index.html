Every minute, on the record.

A candidate pitch, not a final line. Actual is an idea for a passive badge that a crew would want to wear. It would be the worker's own verified timecard, drive time included. Greg is a solo founder, bootstrapping. There is no product, no prototype, and no customers.

Talk to Greg The idea Co-founders

The problem

Small service businesses do not know what each job really costs. True minutes on a property include the drive and the time at the shop. Landscaping, HVAC, plumbing, pest control, house cleaning, in-home care, janitorial, and delivery crews often track that time on memory, paper, or spreadsheets. Overruns stay hidden until month-end.

For the worker

Get paid for every minute you work, drive time included, on a record you own. No daily steps. No phone. Nothing installed on the customer's property. It should not feel like policing.

For the owner

See what every job really costs, proven by the people who did it. Owners get real job costing and protection in a wage dispute, and a way to become the employer of choice.

One arrangement being explored: the worker owns the record, and the employer subscribes to it with the worker's permission.

Design criteria

  • The record comes directly from the person.
  • It covers every day, and the whole day.
  • It is a product, not a service.
  • It stays inside the work relationship.
  • It does not feel like policing.
  • It does not change how work is sold or organized.

Where it stands

  • An idea, at research stage.
  • Greg is looking for a technical co-founder (hardware and software) and a sales or industry co-founder who knows service-business owners.
  • The plan is off-the-shelf hardware and custom software, then a first paid pilot in roughly three to six months.
  • Open questions: who pays for the device first, and what the final pitch should be.

Similar products

Close competitors exist. ZeroBadge, XGen, and Momentum CrewID make wearable badges. Jobber, Workyard, and Timeero use a phone and geofences. The open gap is a truly zero-step badge, the whole day per person, and a record the worker trusts or owns.
