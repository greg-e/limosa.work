Reliable cost tracking for remote workers

Actual is a badge each worker wears on the job. It records the day as it happens, stop by stop, with nothing to tap or clock into. Each worker owns a verified timecard and can check their hours before payroll. With their permission, the same record shows you where each crew worked, what they did, and the exact minutes at every property, so job costs come straight from the work.

Nothing to remember. The badge records everything automatically.

Worker owned. A personal record everyone can trust.
