Proof of Work

On Monday, do you remember where you were and what you were doing at 10:15 last Tuesday? Maybe you wrote it down, can you find it in the next two minutes? When you find it, do you trust what you wrote? Will someone who wasn't there and doesn't know you trust it? If your answer is no, or maybe, then you may have a problem proving your work.

Actual is the solution

Nothing to remember. A personal device that records where you are and what you're doing, on your terms.

Proof anyone can check. The record is proven truth anyone can trust and verify.

Yours to share. You own the record and choose what to share and with whom.
