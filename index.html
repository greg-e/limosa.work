The problem

HVAC, plumbing, pest control, house cleaning, in-home care, delivery, and landscaping businesses do not know how long work takes at each site. Job costing is guesswork. Hours get disputed.

Every current method infers time from something else, or it depends on people doing something every day. The proxies are truck GPS, equipment, and payroll. The daily steps are clock-ins, taps, photos, and entries. Inferred time is wrong. Daily steps get skipped or fudged. Tracking that feels like watching gets resisted.

Contact Deck

Six criteria

  1. The record comes directly from the person, with no inference from proxies.
  2. It covers every day and the whole day, with no sampling.
  3. It's a product, not a service.
  4. It stays inside the company, with nothing on customer property and no insurer or partner in the middle.
  5. It doesn't feel like policing.
  6. It doesn't change how work is sold or organized.

It needs no daily human steps.

The proposed solution

A worker wears a passive badge. It records time on site every day, all day, with no clock-ins, taps, photos, or apps. The record is the worker's own verified timecard. With the worker's permission, the business uses it for job costing and as proof of hours.
