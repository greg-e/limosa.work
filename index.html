Know how long every job takes.

Actual is a badge each worker wears on the job. It records the whole workday, from the first stop to the last, with nothing to tap or clock into. Each worker gets a verified timecard they own. With their permission, you use it to see what every job really costs.

Where a clock-in loses the day

  1. The day moves. Crews visit several properties, and one clock-in can't show how long each took.
  2. Job costing leans on memory. When minutes per property aren't recorded, estimates fill the gap.
  3. Questions surface at payroll. By then the week is over and hard to reconstruct.
  4. Every method needs someone to act. Busy people forget to tap, and that's normal.
