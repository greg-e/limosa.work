Actual is a research-stage idea. A passive badge records time on site as the worker's own verified timecard, for service businesses. There's no product and no customers yet.

Contact Deck

The problem

HVAC, plumbing, pest control, house cleaning, in-home care, delivery, and landscaping businesses do not know how long the work takes at each site. Job costing is guesswork. Hours get disputed.

The idea

The worker wears a passive badge. It records time on site every day, all day. There are no clock-ins, taps, photos, or apps.

The record belongs to the worker as their own verified timecard. With the worker's permission, the business uses it for job costing and as proof of hours.

What it won't do

No cameras. No truck GPS. Nothing is installed on the customer's property. It does not change how the work is sold or scheduled. It should not feel like surveillance.

Why each side would want it

Workers get proof of the hours they are paid for.

Owners get real job costs and protection in wage disputes. The record is an edge in hiring.
